The sea is ringed in fire from shore to shore
The night is bright as any summer noon
The rockets tear the dark above the bay
And the water throweth back a burning moon

The old ones dance who have not danced in years
The young ones kiss beneath the burning sky
The horn goes round, the accordion doth wail
And no one on this coast tonight shall cry

This is ours! This fire! This shore!
This is the birthright of the northern-born!

We set the sea alight tonight!
We crown the water all in flame!

And the fire on the water burneth true!
