The long day loosens at the water's edge
The birch doth cast a longer shadow now
We gather on the stones where we were born
Beneath the greying northern evening sky

Sumar snýr — the summer turneth slow
The light that would not sleep begins to close its eye
One last evening the whole coast doth hold its breath
Before the fires, before the long goodbye

Vér kveikjum eldana — we shall light the fires
Ljós á vatni — light upon the sea!

Come down! Come down to the water and the stones!
Come down where the whole of the coast doth gather in!
For the dark is patient — but so are we!
One more night! One more night of light — begin!
Heyr oss, Skogarmóðir!
The summer thou didst give!
One last evening we shall hold it!
One last evening we shall live!

The nets are drawn, the boats are turned to shore
The children carry driftwood down the sand
The old ones bring the lanterns from the sheds
And no word passeth — all doth understand
