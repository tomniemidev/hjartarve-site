From northern skerries down the long grey coast
From every pier and every jut of stone
A hundred hands do strike a hundred flames
And the dark doth learneth it shall not reign alone

The boats go out with lanterns at their prows
The children run with sparklers on the sand
And bonfire after bonfire crowns the shore
Till the whole of the northland burning stand

Bálit brenna! — the bonfires burneth high!
Eldr á vatni! — fire upon the sea!

Light them! Light them! Every fire on the shore!
Light them till the night doth turneth day!
Kveikið! Kveikið! Bál við bál!
Let the whole coast catch and blaze away!
Eldr! Eldr! Ljós á vatni!
Strike the spark and let it climb!
For the summer's end deserveth fire
And we light it one last time!

The horn goes round from hand to weathered hand
The first sparks climb and scatter on the wind
The elders nod, the young ones cannot wait
And every heart along the coast doth grin

Now the sea is ringed in fire!
Now the night begins!
Eldr á vatni! Eldr á vatni!
