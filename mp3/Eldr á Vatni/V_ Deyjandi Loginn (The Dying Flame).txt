The bonfires burneth low along the coast
The lanterns on the boats do gutter out
The children sleep, the horn is set aside
And the first cold wind of autumn moves about

Haust kemr — the autumn it is coming
The dark that we defied doth take the field
The embers redden, blacken, fall to ash
And the summer that we held we now must yield

Deyjandi loginn! The dying flame!

Allt fýkr — all doth blow away!

And we standeth on the cold wet stone, alone!
