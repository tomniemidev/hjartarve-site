We taketh one small ember from the fire
Before the last of it doth fade to grey
We wrap it warm and hold it to the chest
And we carry it into the dark away

Through all the dark and all the biting winter
Through every night the north shall ever know
Through frost that cracketh stone and freezeth breath
We keep the ember burning, soft and slow

Vér segjum eigi farvel — we say not farewell!
Vér heitum — we do vow!

For when the ice doth break and spring returneth!
And when the sun climbeth back into the sky!
We shall returneth to these self-same stones!
And we shall light the sea again — and never say goodbye!
Skál til sumars! Skál til ljóss!
Skål to every summer yet to be!
We keep the ember, we keep the flame!
And the fire on the water shall burn free!

It knoweth not the ember in our keeping
It knoweth not that we shall bring the sun

We yield the summer, yea
But we keep a single ember
Against the coming grey
And we shall — we shall — RETURN!
