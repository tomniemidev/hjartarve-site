But hear me now, beneath the crack and roar
Beneath the rockets bursting overhead
There is a sorrow woven in this joy
For every flame we light doth light the dead

Sumar deyr — the summer it is dying
Each spark that climbs doth climbeth toward its fall
The brightest fire is the fire that endeth
And we who light it brightest know it all

Allt fýkr — all doth blow away
Even this light... even this night... even we!

The fire we raise against the coming dark
Is the very thing that showeth us the dark!

And we love it all the same!
